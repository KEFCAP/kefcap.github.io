HOW IT WORKS — This dashboard tracks the Composer "Roth IRA" account starting Aug 1, 2026; all earlier history is excluded by design. The performance line is the time-weighted return (TWR), which strips out the effect of weekly deposits — it measures how well the strategies traded, not how much cash went in. Portfolio value, by contrast, grows with both returns and deposits. Returns are benchmarked against SPY and QQQ over the same dates. The range buttons (1W … ALL) reslice the chart and the period-stats table beneath it, which shows cumulative return, max drawdown, and annualized Sharpe (mean ÷ stdev of daily returns × √252, risk-free 0%) for all three over exactly that window. Because the tracking window starts Aug 1, 2026, ranges longer than the available history clamp to that start and will show identical numbers until more history accumulates. Symphony stats (return, Sharpe, drawdown) come from Composer's deposit-adjusted per-symphony series. Risk metrics (Sharpe, max drawdown, std dev) are Composer's all-time account metrics. On this refresh the chart and period stats run through 2026-09-25, the most recent trading day — 27 September is a Saturday. All three lines cover identical trading days, and the portfolio value, latest move and risk metrics in the cards above are live as of the 2026-09-27 pull.
